HOW TO SURVIVE

W A S DMove
SHIFTSprint — costs breath, and it hears you
CTRLCrouch — slow, quiet, small
FFlashlight — light finds fragments, and it finds you
ETake fragment
ESCPause
  1. Collect eight fragments. They are nailed to trees, walls and ruins. Your flashlight makes them glow at distance.
  2. Every fragment wakes it further. It moves faster, closer, and more often. Eight is not a countdown to safety — it is a countdown to the exit.
  3. Never hold your gaze. Looking at it fills the static. When the static fills, the tape ends.
  4. Break line of sight and keep moving. Trees, walls and darkness cut the signal. Standing still does not.
  5. Kill the light to hide. A dark screen is safer than a bright corpse — but the fragments stay hidden too.
